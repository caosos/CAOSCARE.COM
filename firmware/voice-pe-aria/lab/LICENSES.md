# Wake Word Lab — data, tool and model licensing

**Bottom line:**
- **Every model trained in this lab is RESEARCH-ONLY and is NOT commercially
  releasable.** Their negative and background training data include sets
  marked non-commercial, or with unclear rights.
- No model here may ship in release firmware.
- A commercial model needs the separate commercially-cleared pipeline below,
  plus legal review.

Records were taken from the source files and model cards on 2026-10-03/04.
They are not legal advice.

## Path to a commercially releasable model (not started)

1. Replace the negatives and backgrounds with sources that permit commercial
   use. For example:
   - LibriSpeech or Common Voice (CC BY / CC0) for speech negatives;
   - MUSAN (CC BY 4.0) for music and noise;
   - DEMAND (CC BY-SA 3.0; share-alike effect on model weights needs legal
     review);
   - self-recorded room and TV audio with signed releases.
2. Confirm the generator checkpoint's terms in writing, or synthesise
   positives with a TTS whose terms allow commercial training use.
3. Add consented real recordings of residents and staff saying the phrase, in
   real rooms.
4. Keep the commercial pipeline in a separate directory with its own manifest.
   Nothing from the research sets may be mixed in.
5. Get legal review of the final data manifest before any release build.
