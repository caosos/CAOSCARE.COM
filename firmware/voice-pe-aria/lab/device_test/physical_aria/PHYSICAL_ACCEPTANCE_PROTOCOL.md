## After Michael authorizes flashing

1. Record the device's current firmware version and wake word in Home Assistant.
2. Flash `firmware.factory.bin` over USB as in `../../../README.md` → "Flash / recovery".
3. Check in Home Assistant: the device reports version `26.9.0-lab.physical_aria`; the wake-word
   select lists Hey Aria, Hey Sivia, Hey Callista; check the boot log for `micro_wake_word` errors.
4. Recovery to stock: the official installer, as in the README.

## Setup

- Place the Voice PE where a resident's unit would sit (dresser/nightstand, ~1 m high). Record the
  room size, furnishing and the TV's distance from the device.
- Test **one phrase at a time**: select it in Home Assistant, leave the other two unselected.
- Rotate the order of phrases between sessions (A-S-C, S-C-A, C-A-S) so fatigue and time of day
  don't favour one.
- Start each phrase's block at **Slightly sensitive**. Only change sensitivity as a separate,
  labelled block, and record it on every row.
- Speakers: record an ID (no names), age band, sex, accent, dentures y/n, hearing aid y/n. Read the
  pronunciation once, then let people say it their own way; record the form used.
- Leave ~5 s between attempts. A wake = the device's LED/listening response.

## Conditions (10 attempts per condition, per phrase, per speaker)

| Code | Condition | Distance | Position | TV |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | quiet | 1 m | seated, facing device | off |
| Q2 | quiet | 2 m | seated, facing | off |
| Q4 | quiet | 4 m | standing, facing | off |
| FA | facing away | 2 m | seated, back to device | off |
| BS | from bed / seated position | bed or chair, ~2–3 m | lying or seated as a resident would | off |
| TL | TV low | 2 m | seated, facing | on, low volume |
| TN | TV normal | 2 m | seated, facing | on, normal volume |
| TD | TV dialogue-heavy | 2 m | seated, facing | on, normal volume, news / talk show |
| CS | casual speech | 2 m | seated, facing | off — said mid-sentence, e.g. "oh, hey Aria, what time is it" |
| SV | softer voice | 2 m | seated, facing | off — deliberately quiet voice |
| RN | repeated natural attempts | any | as it happens | as it happens — the speaker repeats naturally after a miss, as they would in real life; count every attempt |

Record the TV's measured level at the device (dB) for TL/TN/TD if a meter is available.

## Wrong-phrase wakes (per phrase, quiet, 1 m)

With one phrase selected, say each of these 3 times; any wake counts as a wrong-phrase wake. Note
which phrase caused it.

- the other two candidates' phrases;
- for Hey Aria: area, "hey area", Maria, "hey Maria", Ari, "hey Ari", Ariel, Daria, Victoria;
- for Hey Sivia: Sylvia, "hey Sylvia", Olivia, Siri, Syria, severe, trivia, "see via";
- for Hey Callista: Calista, Krista, Crystal, Melissa, Clarissa, Christmas;
- launcher alone: "hey", "hey there".

## Unsolicited wakes (no one says any wake phrase)

| Soak | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Overnight | 8 h | room as normal, TV off |
| TV | 4 h | TV at normal volume — news, game shows, movies, commercials; nobody in the room |
| Daytime | 8–12 h | normal activity: TV, conversation, phone calls, visitors |

Run each soak with one phrase selected (rotate phrases across days). Record every wake with its
time and, if known, what was playing or being said.

## Recording

Use `PHYSICAL_ACCEPTANCE_RECORD.csv` (one row per condition block). Columns:

`date, session, speaker_id, phrase_selected, sensitivity, condition_code, distance_m, position,
tv_level, pronunciation_used, attempts, successful_wakes, misses, wrong_phrase_wakes,
wrong_phrase_said, unsolicited_wakes, soak_hours, notes`

- `misses = attempts − successful_wakes`.
- `wrong_phrase_wakes` only on wrong-phrase rows; `unsolicited_wakes` + `soak_hours` only on soak
  rows.

## What decides the result

Michael decides from the record. For each phrase, report per condition: catch = successful
wakes ÷ attempts; wrong-phrase wakes; unsolicited wakes per hour (overnight, TV, daytime
separately). A phrase that misses often in BS/SV/TD, or wakes unsolicited during the TV soak, is
not resident-ready regardless of its lab numbers.
