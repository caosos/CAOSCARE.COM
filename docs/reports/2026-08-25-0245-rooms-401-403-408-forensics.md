## Practical root-cause / deployment-requirement note (Michael's field test)

Michael independently reported that running the eMeet as a complete,
self-contained speakerphone (**microphone AND speaker both on the eMeet**)
on this same EliteDesk produced a materially cleaner live conversation than
the earlier split configuration (eMeet microphone, separate soundbar
output). This is recorded here as **strongly supported practical root
cause / deployment requirement based on controlled configuration behavior**
— not as laboratory proof of the eMeet's internal DSP/AEC behavior, which
CAOSCARE's telemetry cannot observe (see finding K above). The mechanism is
consistent with the research already on record in this issue: the eMeet is
a conferencing speakerphone with its own onboard echo-cancellation DSP
designed around a closed loop where it owns both the far-end playback and
the near-end capture; routing Aria's audio through a separate speaker
breaks that closed loop and leaves Chrome's software AEC to handle
substantially more of the acoustic echo alone. **Production/test-room
deployment requirement going forward: one eMeet unit serving as both
microphone and speaker per room**, not split input/output hardware.
