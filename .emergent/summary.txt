**original_problem_statement**: 
I'm building CAOS Care for senior living centers. I want to create a kiosk-style AI interface on a tablet connected to an RF receiver/transmitter to track residents using existing 900 MHz wearable pendants. The AI needs to comfort residents waiting for help, alert staff (who have tablets/pagers), track geolocation inside the building, and eventually utilize AI-vision glasses/earbuds (Vuzix M4000) to guide visually impaired residents. The tablet will also act as a central hub for smart room devices (AC, heater, TV, lights) via BLE/WiFi/RF. 

PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:
- MVP: AI Resident Companion (Claude Sonnet 4.5 + OpenAI Whisper/TTS).
- Auth: JWT + Emergent-managed Google Auth.
- Staff & Admin Dashboards with geolocation tracking, movement timelines, and insights/pattern detection.
- Kiosk Mode with huge buttons for emergency + smart room control + continuous voice loop with Barge-in and Auto-sleep capabilities.
- Hardware integrations: Android Bridge (for RF pendants), Wearables, Smart Room Devices, and AI Vision Glasses.
- Long-term Memory Server: Python-backed memory layer so the AI remembers details about residents permanently.
- Clinician Event Registry: Track alert categories, durations, and AI summaries for medical analytics.
